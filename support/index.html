Report an ad

If you saw an ad in Street Legends that was offensive, misleading or not suitable for the game, please tell us. Email streetdicesupport@gmail.com with:

  • what the ad showed or which company it was for,
  • roughly when you saw it, and
  • a screenshot, if you have one.

We'll block it in our ad settings. Many ads also have a small AdChoices icon in a corner, which lets you hide that ad or tell Google why it's a problem.

Common questions

Is the cash real? Can I buy cards?

No and no. Cash only exists inside Street Legends. You earn it by playing and spend it on card levels, Bling and clothes. Cards, Bling and cash are never for sale, and you can't cash anything out.

How are the card picks after a win chosen?

Each of the three cards is drawn with fixed odds by rarity, which are listed in the game under Settings > How to Play. Picking a card you already have levels it up. Street corner events never involve chance: every choice says exactly what you'll get.

How do combos work?

Every card has a crew badge in its top corner: Bruisers, Smooth or Talkers. Play two cards from the same crew in a row and you get a bonus: 3 damage, 3 Cover or an extra card. A card that would combo glows in your hand.

Where's the Endless Tour?

It unlocks once you beat King Malibu, the boss of Los Angeles. You'll find it on the Streets map.

When does the Daily Challenge change?

At midnight UTC, the same moment for every player. The Today tab shows how long is left. You can retry as often as you like; your best score counts, and the cash prize is paid for your first win each day. Bling and Second Wind are off in the Daily Challenge so the leaderboard stays fair.

My achievements aren't showing in Game Center.

Make sure you're signed in to Game Center in the iOS Settings app. Trophies you earn while signed out are kept in the game (on the Today tab) and sent to Game Center the next time you open Street Legends signed in.

I bought Remove Ads on another device, or reinstalled the app.

Go to Settings > Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account. Remove Ads turns off the ads between fights. Ads that give a reward only play when you choose to watch one, so they stay available.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

For tracking, open the iOS Settings app and go to Privacy & Security > Tracking. In regions where Google asks for consent, Street Legends also shows Settings > Privacy Choices. The privacy policy has the details.

How do I move my progress to a new iPhone?

Your game is included when you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, so restoring that backup to a new phone brings it along. We never receive your progress, so we can't restore it for you.
