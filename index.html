Build a crew. Run the streets.

A turn-based card battler with a cartoon street crew. Fight your way through six cities, from Brooklyn to Los Angeles, and take down the boss at the end of each one.

What's in it

Fights you can plan. Each turn you draw 5 cards and get 3 Hype to spend. Your foe shows its next move above its head, so you know when to hit hard and when to put up Cover.

Crew combos. Every card belongs to a crew: Bruisers, Smooth or Talkers. Play two from the same crew in a row for a bonus.

19 crew cards to collect. Pick a new card after every win, level your favorites up, and at level 5 they turn gold and cost less to play.

Bosses with Bling. Every city ends with a boss who gets fired up at half health. Beat one for the first time and they drop Bling, which gives you an edge in every fight.

Something new every day. A daily stash, three missions and the Daily Challenge: the same fight for every player, with a twist on the rules and a Game Center leaderboard.

The Endless Tour. Beat the final boss to unlock it. Foe after foe, your health carries over, and every stop is tougher.

Your own fighter. Pick your skin, hair and clothes, just for looks.

Earned, never sold

Cards, Bling and cash are only ever earned by playing. They're never for sale, and the odds for the card picks after a win are shown right in the game.

Street Legends is free and shows ads. Ads that give you a reward are always optional, and a one-time purchase removes the ads between fights. Read the privacy policy
